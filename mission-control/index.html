About this screen

Positions are computed live in your browser with SGP4 from public orbit data (CelesTrak, epoch ). Accurate to about a kilometre, so close approaches are screening flags for operators, not collision predictions. “In range” means Sentinel-1 is within 600 km of Kerala, close enough for its side-looking radar; actual acquisitions follow ESA’s plan.
