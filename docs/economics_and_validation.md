## Kerala 2018: Post Disaster Needs Assessment (UN, World Bank, EU, under Government of Kerala)
- Damages ~₹10,557 crore + losses ~₹16,163 crore = **~₹26,720 crore total disaster effects**
- Recovery needs ~₹31,000 crore
- Worst-hit districts: Alappuzha, Ernakulam, Idukki, Kottayam, Pathanamthitta, Thrissur, Wayanad
- Agriculture chapter starts ~p.174 (flooded hectares not yet extracted)

## Karnataka 2019
- CM's request, 10 Aug 2019: **3,22,448 ha** farmland damaged by waterlogging/floodwater
- Later preliminary assessment: **6.9 lakh ha** agriculture + horticulture crop loss
- Quote as a range with dates (the count grew as assessment continued)
- Centre approved additional **₹1,869.85 crore** from NDRF for Karnataka 2019 floods
- These are statewide totals: only comparable if all affected districts are mapped

## PMFBY rules (official operational guidelines, sections 12-13)
- Sum insured per farmer = Scale of Finance (or Notional Average Yield × MSP/farm-gate price) **per hectare** × insured area
- Farmer premium cap (% of sum insured, or actuarial rate if lower):
  - Kharif food grains & oilseeds: **2%**
  - Rabi food grains & oilseeds: **1.5%**
  - Annual commercial / horticultural: **5%**
- Use: payout = flooded insured hectares × sum insured per hectare; premium benchmark
