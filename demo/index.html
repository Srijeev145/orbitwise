Five stages, one command

How the two tracks fit together

Track ASentinel-1 radar→Flood map→Flooded farmland→Evidence for insurer
Track BScreen the satellites→Score risk 2 days ahead→Operator verifies→Keep imagery flowing

Track A depends on a few radar satellites. In 2016 debris damaged Sentinel-1A; in 2021 Sentinel-1B failed (a power fault), and India went from being imaged about every 6 days to every 12.

Why it matters

  • ₹26,720 crore damages and losses from the Kerala 2018 floods (Post Disaster Needs Assessment).
  • 3.2–6.9 lakh ha of Karnataka farmland hit in 2019; the count grew for weeks as surveys continued.
  • PMFBY claims are due within 2 months of crop-cutting surveys and harvest (PIB, 19 Jul 2022).

Stage 1 · Track A

Does the flood AI work?

Trained on Sen1Floods11: radar images from 11 floods on 6 continents where experts drew the flood by hand. Scored by water IoU: overlap with the experts’ water divided by everything either side called water (1.0 = perfect).

Fixed threshold Otsu (standard) Our AI Hybrid (AI + Otsu)

Test floods

The three methods

  • Fixed threshold: any pixel darker than −20 dB is water.
  • Otsu: picks a dark/bright cut-off separately for each image, adapting to each scene.
  • Our AI: Gradient Boosting on 8 numbers per pixel: two radar bands (VV, VH), their difference, neighbourhood averages and texture.
  • Hybrid: 80% the AI’s probability + 20% Otsu’s per-image vote. The weight was chosen by hiding one whole flood event at a time, so it is tuned for floods the model has never seen.

The AI alone is below Otsu on Indian floods and on Bolivia. The hybrid ties Otsu on Indian floods (0.715 vs 0.716) and closes most of the Bolivia gap, while staying ahead on test floods.

Method & limits

What is built, and what is not

Data

  • Sentinel-1 RTC radar (Microsoft Planetary Computer), orbit track 165: before = median of 23 Apr, 5 May, 17 May 2018; after = 21 Aug 2018.
  • ESA WorldCover 2021 land use (farmland = class 40).
  • Sen1Floods11 hand-labelled flood imagery (446 chips).
  • ESA Kelvins Collision Avoidance Challenge (162,634 warnings, 13,154 events).
  • CelesTrak public orbits: Sentinel-1, Cartosat, 4 debris clouds, 15,829 active satellites.

Built and demonstrated

  • Flood AI trained and scored against two baselines.
  • Kerala 2018 flood mapped two ways, with farmland overlay.
  • Simulated payout trigger on ~1 km cells.
  • Collision-risk model 2 days ahead on real ESA warnings.
  • 72-hour screen of 11 satellites against 14,334 objects.

Not claimed

  • Flood exposure is not measured crop loss; ₹ figures use an assumed sum insured.
  • No real policies, payments or village boundaries.
  • The AI–radar cross-check and risk pricing are proposed next steps.
  • Screening flags are not collision predictions; no satellite control.
  • Sentinel-1B failed from a power fault, not debris.
